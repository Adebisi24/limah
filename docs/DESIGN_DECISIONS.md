# Latest design decisions

Warm editorial palette, serif headings, offwhite backgrounds. Use the chosen `home & living` masthead and full footer across templates. Desktop 1440px, mobile 390px. Brand name remains provisional.

1. Homepage: complete editorial homepage, rooms, organization, shopping, newsletter and footer.
2. Inspiration: 15 ideas; desktop idea images centered at 840px. Related bedroom ideas: 12; related shopping: 8. Four columns on desktop, image/title side-by-side on mobile. No table of contents.
3. Shopping landing: editorial stories, four shopping formats, shop by room, Shop the Look, guides and latest stories. No raw product catalog or deals section.
4. Shopping Finds: 15 products. Centered retailer button immediately after image. Remove pros/cons, Best For and Details; retain Why We Like It. Related finds: 12; inspiration: 8. Four desktop columns; side-by-side mobile cards.
5. Best Products: seven recommendations, comparison, methodology and advice. Full product sections support 1–3 centered retailer buttons; mobile stacks them. Quick Picks always use one button. Related shopping: 12; inspiration: 8; four desktop columns and side-by-side mobile cards. Keep pros/cons and details here.
6. Buying Guide: education first, three supporting product examples, sizing diagram and checklists. Full footer.
7. Shop the Look: remove About This Look, How to Recreate This Style, Where to Start, product-group headings and pros/cons. Keep the room hero, Quick Shop and similar-look labels. Center retailer buttons. More Looks to Shop: eight cards, four columns on desktop and side-by-side on mobile. Plan Your Room: eight text-only links with arrows, no images. Keep Why It Works and Look For/Details.
8. Bedroom Hub: ideas, styles, organization, advice, Shop the Look, shopping finds, picks, buying guides, latest stories, newsletter and footer.

General product order unless overridden above: product name, image, price/retailer button, pros/cons, explanation, best for/details. If no verified price, use Check Price at Retailer. Exact and similar products must be distinguished. No fabricated testing claims. No hotspots for V1.

Articles are reused through room/style relationships rather than duplicated by hubs. Style landing pages require sufficient content before becoming public SEO destinations.
